Learning & revision sessions

Choose an exercise and copy the prompt into your preferred AI chat. At the end, say finish: the AI writes your revision document. Save that Markdown wherever you want. On a revisit, paste the saved document into the AI chat when asked.

This page does not receive chat history or save revision documents. Optional browser progress is separate. DSA learners can open the topic companions; LLD learners can open the pattern and design companions. HLD learners can open the system design companions. Python, HTTP, SQL and framework learners can open their track guides.

Exercise titles may reveal the topic; this is not a blind assessment. You can switch modes in the chat.
